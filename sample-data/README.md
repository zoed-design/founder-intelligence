# Reviewed public demo samples

These are selected, de-identified extracts of real project material supplied by the founder. The project address and professional names are presentation aliases. They support one narrow prototype case, not a complete project file or a live building-compliance assessment.

## Source files

- Project schedule extract: task, stage and planned-date columns from the supplied Kira project schedule, with original row numbers. Supplier names, private comments and source workbook metadata are excluded.
- Approval drawing extracts: crops of the roof plan A 1.04 and west elevation A 2.01 from original CDC set pages 10 and 15, issue A2 dated 8 August 2025. Identifier-bearing title blocks, names, addresses and document identifiers are excluded. Drawing values are retained.
- Structural notes extract: timber notes T1-T7 cropped from original structural set page 1, S01. It does not authorise a roof-height change.
- OC document checklist: the supplied one-page checklist, with a fictional certifier practice. Requirements are reproduced as supplied, without confirming current legal or technical applicability.
- Site conversation extract: transcribed text using Kira and Mark as presentation names. Images, avatars, group membership and timestamps are excluded.

The partly anonymised full approval set, full structural set, original workbook and chat screenshot are not published in this public pack. Other private source records are not bundled. This folder contains no credentials or live account connections.

## What the source check establishes

The elevation labels show top of wall 48.10, roof FFL 47.30 and level 1 FFL 44.60. Arithmetic gives 3.50 m to top of wall, 2.70 m to roof FFL and a difference of 800 mm. The conversation also requests 800 mm in the middle. The roof plan and conversation both state a 5 degree fall. These support a takeoff-review flag, not automatic approval or a uniform wall height. Openings and the local roof profile still need review.

The roof-covering schedule entry, source row 78, has no recorded start or finish date. No actual-completion, inspection-signoff or loan-claim status is supplied. Schedule stage titles such as full structural frame completed are labels, not proof that the work has occurred.

## Client communication facts

The team has a roof-detail query to review. The records do not confirm the roof-covering date, readiness or inspection sign-off. A client draft should report the review and promise a confirmed date only when available.

## Public story context

A story may describe careful drawing review and construction coordination at the Coastal Duplex. Do not assert a completed framing or roofing milestone from this pack, or publish technical measurements, site identifiers, names or claim details in the story.

## Prototype method

A reviewed structured data file drives arithmetic, schedule-gap checks, audience-specific drafts and reference lookup. Changing the source values changes these checks. Uploaded photos are session attachments and are not analysed. Digger reviews all drafts before local approval and download. No message, reminder or social post is dispatched. Role views in this public static prototype do not provide production authentication.
