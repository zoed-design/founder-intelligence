Explore what’s possible for your home.

A free consultation would help you discuss your goals, understand the information needed and identify which planning pathways merit a closer look.

  • Your goals: renovate, extend, rebuild or explore a duplex.
  • Available site information: zoning, dimensions, planning certificate and known constraints.
  • Questions for a planner, designer or certifier, and the next steps to investigate.

An address, block size and frontage are a starting point. Site conditions, restrictions and the proposed design also matter.
