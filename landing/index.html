Product mission & prototype preview

Empower your
business to scale.

Turn everyday data updates into intelligent knowledge the whole team can use.

A connected construction workspace designed to bring conversations, site updates, project records and compliance references together. Support the team with employee coaching, clear client updates and Client Stories, with source references and human review.

The working prototype demonstrates one small, photo-led workflow within this wider vision.

The coordination burden

Knowledge gets scattered.
The founder becomes the router.

01

Knowledge trapped with the founder

Project context lives across chats and folders. The same questions keep coming back to one person.

02

Project and compliance friction

Employee guidance, drawings and reference checks are scattered, making it harder to identify issues early.

03

Client and brand updates get delayed

Milestone updates, weekly reports and marketing stories compete with daily firefighting.

Expected value of the product mission

More capacity for the work that matters.

01

Scale the business,
not just maintain it

Reduce routine coordination so the founder can focus on estimating, relationships and business growth.

02

Improve project management
and compliance accuracy

Provide useful, traceable references for the team and clients. Earlier checks may help identify potential non-compliant work before it is covered up, reducing issues that could delay loan drawdowns, affect cashflow or complicate the Occupancy Certificate process.

03

Keep client and
brand updates consistent

Support client communication at key milestones and through weekly progress updates, with consistent marketing stories throughout the week.

These are expected benefits, not measured outcomes of the current prototype.

The intended architecture

A specialist crew.
Shared context.

Lyra collects and organizes. Bower finds references and coordinates next steps. Each role works with the context it needs, while Digger retains human oversight.

Digger Site Manager
Shares site context and reviews the team's work.
Lyra Collect & organize
Turns fragmented information into findable Wiki knowledge.
Bower Find, reference & allocate
Finds relevant information and prepares coordinated next actions.
Roo Employee Coach
Explains the steps and flags questions for Digger to review.
Tally Client
Prepares clear client progress and milestone updates.
Kooka Client Stories
Develops stories about the client's project journey.

What the prototype demonstrates

Photo input, four role views, prepared draft templates, keyword-based example references, session-only tasks and reminder drafts, editing, approval and download.

It does not analyze images, extract facts from project files, verify compliance, run a live generative-AI service or send communications. The same prepared framing scenario is used for the demonstration.

Browser workflow checks have been completed. Independent tester feedback is still to be recorded.

Our data

Real-world context.
Clear source boundaries.

Own project material

Founder-supplied site photos, a schedule, approval and structural drawings, roof markup and a site / administration chat record informed the use case. Original project records are not published on this page.

Public reference families

NCC / ABCB Housing Provisions, the NSW Housing Code and Randwick Council's LEP and DCP are intended knowledge sources. Full ingestion and project-specific applicability are not verified in this prototype.

Illustrative prototype data

Fictional identifiers, example source notes, prepared templates and illustrative artwork support the current demonstration. They are not verified regulatory extracts or assessments of an uploaded image.

Assumptions: the prototype uses one prepared framing scenario. Additional Council and authorised Australian Standards materials can be added after they are supplied and reviewed.

Privacy

Reviewed material.
Limited personal information.

This page uses fictional project identifiers, product screenshots and illustrative artwork. It publishes no original client records, real client names or exact real project addresses. Team attribution is limited to the names and professional contributions supplied for publication.

Real project assets for later presentations still require manual review and de-identification. The prototype's text checks do not automatically anonymise images or all project information.
