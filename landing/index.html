Product mission & prototype preview

Empower your

You started a building business to grow, but with every question, client update and site fire landing on you, there’s no time left for the work that moves it forward.

Turn scattered conversations and project updates into shared knowledge that supports employee coaching, traceable project decisions, and consistent client and brand updates.

The coordination burden

Knowledge gets scattered.
The founder becomes the router.

01

Knowledge trapped with the founder

Chats, emails and site decisions pile up faster than the founder can organise and label them in a shared Wiki. Admin staff lack the full picture, don’t know where to find the right answers, and are buried in daily admin—so every question comes back to the founder.

02

Project and compliance friction

Employee guidance, drawings and reference checks are scattered, making it harder to identify issues early.

03

Client and brand updates get delayed

Milestone updates, weekly reports and marketing stories compete with daily firefighting.

Expected value of the product mission

More capacity for the work that matters.

01

Support client communication at key milestones and through weekly progress updates, with consistent marketing stories throughout the week.

Current working prototype

Try four everyday workflows.

The working prototype demonstrates a small part of the wider product vision through the four workflows below.

Open the prototype in a new tab ↗

01 · Site Manager

One photo. Three drafts.

  1. As Digger, drop the framing photo into the conversation.
  2. Bower uses the project references to allocate follow-up: Roo prepares employee coaching, Tally prepares a client update, and Kooka drafts a Brand Story for Instagram.
  3. Review the drafts and their sources before approving.

The photo starts the workflow; this prototype does not analyse its pixels.

02 · Message to knowledge

Collect the chat. Coach the admin.

  1. As Digger, choose Add a WhatsApp message and upload the WhatsApp example above.
  2. Review the supplied transcript and select Collect into Wiki. Lyra labels and indexes it.
  3. Open Roo · Employee Coach for the prepared guidance and drawing references. Ask follow-up questions.

Manual import simulates a future WhatsApp connection. Messages remain unverified; no live message is sent.

03 · Client

Ask Tally. See the references.

  1. Open Tally · Client and ask: “When will roof covering finish?” or “When will the entire project finish?”
  2. Try: “What are the site requirements to build a duplex in Randwick Council?”
  3. Open the sources. Bower explains the rules and the further site information needed to assess a Council DA or a CDC, including relevant LMR or Pattern Book provisions.

Dates are projected, and missing dates stay unknown. Planning references do not approve a site.

04 · Junior employee

Prepare the OC documents.

  1. Open Roo · Employee Coach and ask: “How do I prepare the OC documents for this project?”
  2. Review the supplied checklist and source references. Ask: “Do I need a certificate for roof covering?”
  3. Confirm applicable documents, inspections and outstanding items with Digger and the responsible certifier.

This demonstrates preparation guidance. It does not submit an application or issue an Occupation Certificate.

One demo project: 18 Maroubra Street, Maroubra, Sydney (fictional alias). No sign-in is needed. Start with New session; reload or New session clears imported messages and edits. You can also use Wiki → Use supplied site photo / Try supplied WhatsApp message.

Our data

Real-world context.
Clear source boundaries.

Own project material

Founder-supplied site photos, a schedule, approval and structural drawings, roof markup and a site / administration chat record informed the use case. Selected de-identified extracts are available in the prototype. Full source packs, title blocks, private comments and contact details are excluded from publication.

Public planning references

ABCB Housing Provisions (NCC 2022 reference), the NSW Low-Rise Housing Diversity Code and Randwick Council's LEP and DCP are part of a reviewed reference snapshot, alongside NSW LMR and Housing Pattern Book guidance. Bower provides topic-based answers with official links and scope limits. The reference pack was checked on 17 November 2026; it is not a live legal feed or a site assessment. Current legislation links remain available for independent checking where retrieval was blocked.

Reviewed prototype data

Fictional identifiers, reviewed source values, selected project extracts and illustrative product artwork support the demonstration. Schedule answers use the supplied mock-up’s projected dates, with missing milestones and conflicting notes flagged; they are not a regulatory assessment or analysis of an uploaded photo.

Scope: one project only. The schedule is a supplied mock-up with projected dates; a projected OC date is not permission to move in. Additional Council and authorised Australian Standards materials can be added after they are supplied and reviewed.

Privacy
