Product mission & prototype preview

Empower your
business to scale.

You started a building business to grow, but with every question, client update and site fire landing on you, there’s no time left for the work that moves it forward.

Turn scattered conversations and project updates into shared knowledge that supports employee coaching, traceable project decisions, and consistent client and brand updates.

The working prototype demonstrates one small, photo-led workflow within this wider vision.

The coordination burden

Knowledge gets scattered.
The founder becomes the router.

01

Knowledge trapped with the founder

Chats, emails and site decisions pile up faster than the founder can organise and label them in a shared Wiki. Admin staff lack the full picture, don’t know where to find the right answers, and are buried in daily admin—so every question comes back to the founder.

02

Project and compliance friction

Employee guidance, drawings and reference checks are scattered, making it harder to identify issues early.

03

Client and brand updates get delayed

Milestone updates, weekly reports and marketing stories compete with daily firefighting.

Expected value of the product mission

More capacity for the work that matters.

01

Scale the business,
not just maintain it

Reduce routine coordination so the founder can focus on estimating, relationships and business growth.

02

Improve project management
and compliance accuracy

Provide useful, traceable references for the team and clients. Earlier checks may help identify potential non-compliant work before it is covered up, reducing issues that could delay loan drawdowns, affect cashflow or complicate the Occupancy Certificate process.

03

Keep client and
brand updates consistent

Support client communication at key milestones and through weekly progress updates, with consistent marketing stories throughout the week.

These are expected benefits, not measured outcomes of the current prototype.

The intended architecture

A specialist crew.
Shared context.

Lyra collects and organizes. Bower finds references and coordinates next steps. Each role works with the context it needs, while Digger retains human oversight.

Digger Site Manager
Shares site context and reviews the team's work.
Lyra Collect & organize
Turns fragmented information into findable Wiki knowledge.
Bower Find, reference & allocate
Finds relevant information and prepares coordinated next actions.
Roo Employee Coach
Explains the steps and flags questions for Digger to review.
Tally Client
Prepares clear client progress and milestone updates.
Kooka Brand Stories
Develops brand stories from the team’s work and expertise.

Our data

Real-world context.
Clear source boundaries.

Own project material

Founder-supplied site photos, a schedule, approval and structural drawings, roof markup and a site / administration chat record informed the use case. Selected de-identified extracts are available in the prototype. Full source packs, title blocks, private comments and contact details are excluded from publication.

Public reference families

NCC / ABCB Housing Provisions, the NSW Housing Code and Randwick Council's LEP and DCP are intended knowledge sources. These are roadmap references. Current answers use only the supplied project records and imported messages; these public reference families are not searched or verified by the prototype.

Reviewed prototype data

Fictional identifiers, reviewed source values, selected project extracts and illustrative product artwork support the demonstration. Schedule answers use the supplied mock-up’s projected dates, with missing milestones and conflicting notes flagged; they are not a regulatory assessment or analysis of an uploaded photo.

Scope: one project only. The schedule is a supplied mock-up with projected dates; a projected OC date is not permission to move in. Additional Council and authorised Australian Standards materials can be added after they are supplied and reviewed.

Privacy

Reviewed material.
Limited personal information.

This page uses fictional project identifiers, product screenshots and selected de-identified source extracts. Full client records, real client names and exact real project addresses are excluded from publication. Team attribution is limited to the names and professional contributions supplied for publication.

Real project assets for later presentations still require manual review and de-identification. The prototype's text checks do not automatically anonymise images or all project information.
