# Crew roles and information flow

This is the agreed product background for future demo slides. The live panel intentionally uses short labels and arrows rather than the full descriptions below.

## The simple visual

```text
                   New message
                    ↙       ↘
                 Digger ←→ Lyra
                    ↕        ↓ Collect & organize
                  Bower ←→ Wiki
                ↙   ↕   ↘
              Roo  Tally  Kooka
              ↖     ↕     ↗
           Questions ↔ tasks & reminder drafts
```

## Lyra collects and organizes

Lyra turns fragmented information into useful, findable knowledge. Potential inputs include WhatsApp conversations, emails, site photos and videos, progress updates, and recent building and compliance updates.

Lyra groups, labels and links information for the Wiki. When available, labels should include the project, site-update context, date, topic, source and relevant people. Missing context stays unknown rather than being guessed. Original source references should remain attached. Lyra and Digger exchange context so that coordination and organized knowledge stay aligned.

## Bower finds, references and allocates tasks

Bower responds to questions from Digger, Roo, Tally, Kooka or another authorized user. Bower finds the right information, filters out noise and returns the most relevant results with references to the original sources so answers can be checked and traced. Retrieval respects the requester's permitted project and audience. Where evidence is missing or conflicting, the answer should say so.

Bower also turns the relevant context into next actions, allocates them to Roo (contractor admin), Tally (client communication) and Kooka (stories), and prepares reminder drafts. Each role can ask follow-up questions and return its work for review. Digger remains the Site Manager and human review role. The main conversation is **Talk to Bower**, viewed as Digger; the other pages let Roo, Tally and Kooka ask Bower in their own context.

## Current demo scope

The panel illustrates the intended roles and flow. The photo-led app uses fixed templates and fictional reference notes. Bower performs a small audience-scoped lookup of those notes, returns inspectable references, and creates session-only task and reminder drafts. It does not connect to WhatsApp, email, Notion or Drive, send or schedule reminders, ingest live compliance updates, perform image recognition, persist a Wiki, or run live semantic retrieval. Existing audience boundaries, review gates and the general-purpose engine remain in place. These distinctions should stay clear in future slides and videos.

## Agreed visual direction

Use slate and mist blue for the crew panel and Wiki, warm sand and light brown for navigation and headlines, off-white surfaces, and earth-colored borders. Keep Digger's existing animated scene and warm construction accents. Avoid pink or lavender surface fills. Use the same direction for future landing-page, pitch-video and demo-slide work.
