# Planning references in the prototype

Reviewed: **17 November 2026**. Scope: Randwick local controls and selected NSW / ABCB guidance. This is a curated, topic-based reference snapshot, not a live legal engine, general semantic search or site approval service.

## Sources and coverage

The public register is [regulatory-references.json](../sample-data/regulatory-references.json). Each entry has an official URL, publisher, section locator, review date, status and a short evidence summary. Open Wiki → Planning references in the app to inspect it. References are available before uploading a photo and without a Drive or Notion login.

The founder's selected Drive folder and linked Notion regulatory pages informed source selection. Official sources were checked separately. Private strategy notes, other councils' local controls, cloud IDs, access links and original source packs are excluded from publication. The selected schedule and checklist were checked against the existing project extracts; this does not connect or synchronise the cloud folder with the public app.

- NSW LMR summary: conditional dual-occupancy standards, linked to the scope and exclusions guidance. Not universal Randwick minimums or a complete building envelope.
- Randwick LEP: Council overview and links to statutory maps / current written instrument. No parcel zoning or map assessment has been made for the fictional project alias.
- Randwick DCP: Council's version and transition page records Stage 2 commencing 27 July 2026. The linked C1 chapter is used for section navigation only. Its PDF footer retains a Draft label; the Council page is the basis for the version description. No unverified numerical DCP limit is asserted.
- Low-Rise Housing Diversity Code: conditional complying-development pathway; no automatic CDC entitlement.
- NSW Housing Pattern Book: distinct low-rise complying-development and mid-rise DA routes, subject to applicable requirements. No guaranteed approval time is claimed.
- ABCB Housing Provisions: the selected NCC 2022 page is a building-technical reference, not evidence of the applicable edition or an approval for this project.
- Current Housing SEPP, Randwick LEP and Codes SEPP instrument links: **linked-only**. Automated retrieval returned HTTP 403. Full current statutory text was not verified; independently check the current instruments, maps and applicable provisions before using them for an actual proposal.

## Example questions

As Tally or Digger:

- Can I build a duplex in Randwick Council? What are the site requirements?
- What LMR lot area, width, FSR and height standards apply to a dual occupancy?
- Does meeting 450 m² and 12 m mean I automatically qualify for a CDC?
- How does the NSW Housing Pattern Book approval pathway work?
- Which Randwick DCP version and sections should I check?

Answers identify the actual lot, zoning, dimensions, maps, LMR boundary/walking-distance conditions, exclusions and other applicable controls as information still needed. DA and CDC are approval pathways; LMR and Pattern Book provisions need separate applicability checks. These are not four interchangeable approvals. Any potentially relevant policy interaction must be assessed for the actual proposal.

## Implementation boundaries

The registry is versioned with the app. It has no automatic update feed. Imported messages remain unverified and cannot replace official references or project dates. Public planning answers are separate from private project messages and Kooka's Brand Stories. If the registry fails validation or cannot load, planning answers state that the pack is unavailable; the schedule, photo and employee workflows remain usable.

The planning corpus covers selected topics, not every clause or construction compliance requirement. A responsible planner / certifier and Council must confirm the actual approval route and applicable controls. The prototype does not submit applications, book inspections or issue certificates.
