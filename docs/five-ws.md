# Five Ws: scope the problem

**What.** Small businesses create useful knowledge daily in emails, chats, meeting notes, voice messages and SOPs, but little reaches a shared knowledge base. It stays with the founder, so staff, customers and marketing all wait on the founder. Evidence: the hackathon brief; [founder interviews]; [hours per week spent on repeat questions].

**Who.** The founder, who is both the main source and the bottleneck; employees who need answers and procedures; customers who want informed replies and project progress. The founder is the primary user and approver.

**Where.** In the tools the business already uses. The decision step sits between "something useful was said" and "it is in the knowledge base, approved, at the right access level". Outputs land in an internal assistant, a customer service channel and the founder's content channels.

**When.** Continuously, as knowledge is created in every conversation. Pain peaks when a new hire starts, the founder is away, a customer asks for an update, or a process changes and the SOP does not.

**Why.** Founder time goes to repeat questions, new staff ramp slowly, customers get inconsistent answers, and expertise never becomes content. Why now: agents can read across everyday tools and draft knowledge updates, but SMEs lack the onboarding, integration and access controls to use them safely. Why this team: BA discipline plus AI governance means we build the review step, access tiers and audit trail, not just a chatbot.
