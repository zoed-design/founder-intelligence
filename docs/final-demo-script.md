# Final prototype demo recording script

Target: 2 minutes 50 seconds. Speak at a steady pace and perform the actions while narrating. Paste the questions rather than typing them. The spoken script is approximately 310 words.

Before recording, open the Prototype guide and app in separate tabs. Download the framing photo and the latest WhatsApp example from the guide. Start a New session. Keep the four questions below ready to paste.

## 0:00–0:20 — Introduce the problem

Screen: landing page mission, then open the prototype.

“Construction founders and senior managers spend too much time chasing information and answering the same questions. Construction and Contractor Intelligence aims to turn everyday site updates into shared knowledge for the team, clients and brand. This prototype demonstrates a small part of that vision.”

## 0:20–0:55 — One photo, three drafts

Screen: as Digger, upload the framing photo. Show the three drafts, briefly open a source, then open Kooka · Brand Stories to show the actual photo post. Do not download during recording.

“I’m acting as Digger, the site manager. I upload one site photo. Bower uses the available project records to prepare three drafts: employee guidance from Roo, a client update from Tally, and a photo-based brand post from Kooka. The site manager can review the wording and references before anything is used externally.”

## 0:55–1:30 — Message to useful knowledge

Screen: return to Digger. Choose Add a WhatsApp message, select the downloaded WhatsApp example, briefly show its transcript, then Collect into Wiki. Show Takeoff knowledge · SOP draft and its references.

“Next, I upload a site-manager-to-admin WhatsApp example containing a roof discussion and markup. For this supplied example, we use a reviewed transcript. Lyra organises it into a takeoff knowledge draft, linked to the message and drawing references. The value is preserving useful estimating and quantity-ordering guidance for employees. This remains a draft for Digger’s review.”

## 1:30–2:00 — Coach an employee

Screen: open Roo · Employee Coach. Paste: “What OC documents are needed for this project and what certificate is needed for roof covering?” Show the preparation steps and roofing certificate reference; do not read the full checklist.

“A junior employee asks how to prepare the OC documents and which roofing certificate is needed. Roo gives preparation steps and points to the relevant checklist items. The employee can see what to request and what still needs confirmation, rather than simply receiving a document link.”

## 2:00–2:25 — Answer a client from the schedule

Screen: open Tally · Client. Paste: “When will roof and entire project finish?” Briefly expand Sources.

“The client asks about both roofing and overall completion. Tally references the revised project schedule and answers both questions, distinguishing projected dates from confirmed completion. The source remains available to check.”

## 2:25–2:40 — Keep a person in the loop

Screen: as Tally, paste: “Can you confirm the exact tile delivery time on Friday?” Then switch to Digger and show that question in Waiting for Digger review. Leave it pending; do not invent a delivery answer.

“When the records do not confirm an answer, the question goes to Digger’s review queue. Digger can provide a reviewed reply in the same session.”

## 2:40–2:55 — Show the roadmap

Screen: switch to the guide’s Product roadmap section.

“The roadmap is to add runtime AI, richer context, reliable retrieval, persistent storage, connected channels and live weather forecasts. These are future capabilities. Our mission is to empower construction and contractor businesses to scale.”

Stop recording by 2:55. If clicks take longer, skip expanding a source or scrolling the full checklist; keep the spoken explanation short.
