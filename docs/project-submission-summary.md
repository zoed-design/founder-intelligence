# Project Title

Construction and Contractor Intelligence Agent

## Tagline

Empower your business to scale.

## What you will build

We are building a construction knowledge workspace that helps founders spend less time answering the same questions and putting out fires, and more time growing their business.

The product vision brings fragmented conversations, emails, site photos, project documents and regulatory references into shared knowledge the whole team can use. Lyra collects and organizes information into a Wiki. Bower finds relevant information, provides source references and prepares the next steps. Roo coaches employees, Tally supports clear client updates, and Kooka creates Brand Stories. The person acting as Digger, the Site Manager, reviews outputs and decisions before external use.

Our current working prototype demonstrates a small part of this vision using one de-identified project. A framing photo starts three draft responses; a manually imported WhatsApp example becomes searchable session knowledge and employee guidance; clients can ask about projected milestones and selected Randwick planning requirements; and junior staff can prepare OC documents using the supplied checklist and references.

The intended value is more capacity to scale, better project coordination and earlier identification of potential compliance issues, with consistent client and brand communication. These are the outcomes we aim to support as the product develops.

## Prototype scope

The public demo uses reviewed project extracts and a dated official planning-reference snapshot. It has no live WhatsApp, email, Drive or Notion connection and does not analyse photo pixels. Answers are topic-based and source-linked; projected dates are not confirmed completion, and planning references do not establish site approval. A responsible person checks the output.
