# Project Title

Construction and Contractor Intelligence Agent

## Tagline

Empower your construction and contractor business to scale.

## What I will build

I plan to build an intelligent agent that acts as an extension of a construction business founder / senior manager and the company’s shared brain, using AI to turn everyday business updates into useful knowledge for employees, clients and the brand.

The concept is a specialist crew of agents with complementary responsibilities. Some would collect, label and organize fragmented information from conversations, emails, site photos and project documents into a shared Wiki. Others would find relevant information with source references, coordinate tasks, coach employees, prepare client updates and develop brand stories. The founder / senior manager would retain oversight and review important outputs and actions.

For example, a site photo could prompt employee guidance, a client progress update and a draft marketing story. A site conversation could become reusable project knowledge. A junior employee could ask which documents to prepare for an Occupation Certificate, while a client could ask when roofing is expected to finish. Planning questions could draw on official references and identify what further site information is needed.

The aim is to reduce the coordination bottleneck, help the team work with better context and give founders / senior managers more time to lead and grow the business.

## Intended value

1. Scale the business, not just maintain it.
2. Improve project management and compliance accuracy.
3. Keep client and brand updates consistent.

## Banner

The 16:9 concept banner uses the existing cream, warm brown and soft blue palette. An illustrated Site Manager represents the proposed AI agent, with unnamed role illustrations for collecting, finding, coaching, client communication and brand stories. No photographs of people are included.
